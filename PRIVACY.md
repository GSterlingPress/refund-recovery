# Refund Recovery Privacy Policy (submission draft)

Refund Recovery processes Shopify store data only to provide refund/return auditing and monitoring requested by the merchant. The service uses the minimum Shopify permissions required for its features and does not sell merchant or customer data.

Data processed may include order identifiers, product/line-item information, refund and return records, fulfillment state, monetary amounts, and store identity. V1 is read-only and does not issue refunds or change inventory.

Store access credentials must be stored server-side and must not be exposed to browsers or logs. When a merchant uninstalls the app, access is revoked by Shopify. Production deployment must implement Shopify's required privacy/compliance webhooks and a documented deletion process before App Store submission.

Contact and legal entity details must be inserted before publication.
