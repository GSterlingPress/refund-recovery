# Refund Recovery Terms (submission draft)

Refund Recovery is an auditing aid. Findings identify records that may deserve merchant review; they do not guarantee that money or inventory is recoverable and are not accounting, legal, or tax advice.

The merchant remains responsible for refund, return, accounting, and inventory decisions. V1 does not automatically modify Shopify orders, refunds, returns, or inventory.

Paid subscriptions renew through Shopify according to the plan accepted by the merchant. Final legal entity, contact, cancellation, governing-law, and support terms must be completed before publication.
